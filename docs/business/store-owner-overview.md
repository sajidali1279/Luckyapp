## 2. How Customers Earn and Redeem

### Earning Points

Every qualifying purchase earns the customer cashback as Lucky Stop credits, at a percentage that depends on their loyalty tier - plus any category bonus or active offer stacked on top. Rates are configured and can be adjusted by Lucky Stop HQ (not fixed store-side); as a reference point, tiers typically range from **5% at Bronze up to 9% at Platinum**.

**Example:** A Gold-tier customer (say, 7% base) spends $40 on groceries at your store, and groceries carry a +1% category bonus that week: 7% + 1% = 8% effective rate, or 320 credits (1 credit = 1 cent). Credits are credited to their account the moment the employee completes the scan.

Some tiers can also be configured to earn a flat cents-per-gallon bonus on gas/diesel instead of a percentage.

### Loyalty Tiers

Customers move through tiers - **Bronze → Silver → Gold → Diamond → Platinum** - based on their total credits earned over a rolling period. Tier and category rates are configured and owned by Lucky Stop HQ, not visible in your Store Manager portal - contact Cliff Industries if you want current rates for your store.

Higher-tier customers earn faster, which means they visit more frequently. Your store benefits from this automatically.

### Redeeming Credits

Customers redeem credits through the **Catalog** - a list of specific products you offer as free rewards (e.g., a fountain drink, a bag of chips, a coffee). When a customer redeems:

1. They select an item from the catalog in the app
2. The app generates a redemption code that expires within 15 minutes
3. They show the code to your cashier
4. The cashier marks it as redeemed in the app
5. The product is given at no charge

The catalog is managed by Lucky Stop HQ (Super Admin). Contact Cliff Industries if you want to add or change the items available at your location.

## 3. How Your Employees Use the App

Every employee who handles loyalty scans must have their own account. You create and manage employee accounts from your admin portal.

### The Employee Workflow (Every Transaction)

1. Customer opens their app and shows their QR code
2. Employee opens the Lucky Stop app → taps **Scan Customer QR**
3. Employee scans the QR code - the customer's account appears
4. Employee enters the purchase total (in dollars)
5. Employee photographs the receipt - **this step is mandatory and cannot be skipped**
6. Employee taps **Grant Points** - transaction is complete

The receipt photo is required on every scan. This is a fraud protection measure. Transactions without a receipt upload are blocked by the system.

### What Employees Can't Do

- They cannot edit or delete a transaction after it's submitted
- They cannot see other customers' full account details
- They cannot change point balances directly
- They cannot approve redemption codes - that's done separately by the cashier at the register

### Employee Requests and Alerts

Employees have two separate ways to flag things to you, both landing in your **Requests** hub:
- **Stock requests** - specific products they think should be stocked or restocked. Approve to add them straight to your active order list.
- **Alerts** - operational issues (low stock, store supplies, a customer asking for something you don't carry, or a work order like broken equipment), each with a priority level, which you acknowledge with a note once handled.

Customers can also submit product requests directly from their app, which land in the same Requests hub under a separate Products tab.

## 5. Running Promotions and Offers

Offers are time-limited promotions that give customers a higher cashback rate for a set period.

**Example:** "Earn 10% cashback on all hot foods this weekend" - a customer who buys a hot dog and a coffee earns double the usual rate on those items.

### Creating an Offer

1. Go to **Offers** in the admin portal
2. Tap **New Offer**
3. Fill in: offer title, description, bonus rate, start date, end date
4. Optionally attach it to a specific product category
5. Publish - eligible customers at your store receive a push notification immediately

Offers run until their end date or until you manually deactivate them.

### Banners

Banners are promotional images shown in the customer app when they're near your store (within roughly 2 miles). Use these for:

- Seasonal promotions
- New product announcements
- In-store events
- Gas price promotions

Upload a banner image (JPG or PNG, landscape recommended) with a title and optional link. Banners are approved by Lucky Stop HQ before going live to prevent misuse.

## 6. Managing Your Staff

### Adding an Employee

1. Go to **Staff** in the admin portal
2. Tap **Add Staff Member**
3. Enter their name and phone number
4. Select role: **Employee** (cashier) or **Store Manager**
5. The employee receives an OTP on their phone to set up their account

### Removing an Employee

Deactivate their account from the Staff page. Their access is revoked immediately. All their past transactions remain in the system for audit purposes.

### Resetting Access

If an employee loses access to their phone number, contact Cliff Industries - phone number changes on staff accounts require admin-level verification.

### The Audit Log

Every action taken in your store is logged: who scanned what, when, what offers were created, and what changes were made to settings. This log is visible to you and to Lucky Stop HQ. It cannot be edited or deleted.

## 7. Order Lists and Inventory

### Order Lists

The Order List is your store's digital procurement tracker. Add items you need to order, assign quantities and suppliers, and track delivery status.

Employees can submit **item requests** - products they've noticed are low or missing. When you approve a request, it automatically adds to your active order list.

### Inventory Analytics

The Inventory Analytics page shows:

- Sales trends by category (last 7, 30, and 90 days)
- Which categories are growing or declining
- Items that appear frequently in employee requests (signals chronic shortage)
- AI-powered reorder suggestions based on recent transaction data

This is based on loyalty scan data - it reflects what customers are buying when they use the app, not your full POS total.

## 8. Scheduling and Shift Management

### Building a Schedule

1. Go to **Scheduling**
2. Create a **Shift Template** (e.g., Morning 6am–2pm, Evening 2pm–10pm)
3. Assign employees to shifts for the week
4. Publish the schedule - employees see their shifts in the app

### Employee Shift Requests

Employees can submit shift swap or time-off requests through the app. These come to you as notifications, and you approve or decline them from the Scheduling page or your mobile app.

## 9. Analytics and Reporting

### Transaction Analytics

Available from your dashboard home:

- Daily, weekly, and monthly transaction counts
- Total credits issued per period
- Average transaction value among loyalty customers
- New vs. returning loyalty customers
- Top customers by spend (anonymized unless you drill in)

### Leaderboard

The **Staff Leaderboard** ranks your employees by customer rating. Customers can rate the employee who served them after a transaction. This gives you visibility into who your best performers are and where service may need attention.

The **Customer Leaderboard** shows your top-spending loyalty members. Recognizing them (or offering them exclusive perks) helps retain your highest-value customers.
