## 1. Your Role and Responsibilities

As a **Store Manager**, you are responsible for the day-to-day operational management of your assigned Lucky Stop location(s) through the Lucky Stop platform. Your key responsibilities include:

- **Reviewing and approving inventory requests** from employees.
- **Managing your store's procurement order lists** — tracking what needs to be ordered, what has been ordered, and what has been received.
- **Creating and managing store-specific promotional offers** for your location.
- **Managing banners** displayed to customers who shop at your store.
- **Managing employee schedules** — creating shift templates and approving/denying time-off requests.
- **Reviewing transactions** — auditing purchases, reviewing flagged transactions, and ensuring receipt quality.
- **Monitoring store performance** through your dashboard and analytics.
- **Managing store operational requests** (supply issues, work orders) submitted by employees.
- **Participating in store team chat** with your employees.
- **Responding to customer product requests** for your store.

**What Store Managers cannot do:**
- Access data from other stores (unless assigned to multiple stores).
- Create or delete customer accounts.
- Create platform-wide (all stores) offers — only store-specific offers.
- Access billing management.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Logging In

Your account was created by Lucky Stop HQ (Super Admin). You will receive your phone number registration and initial PIN from your administrator.

**First login:**
1. Download the Lucky Stop app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
2. Open the app and tap **Log In**.
3. Enter your registered phone number.
4. Enter the initial PIN provided by your administrator.
5. **Change your PIN immediately:** Go to Profile → Change PIN → Set your personal 4-digit PIN.

### 2.2 Your Manager Dashboard

When you log in, you are taken directly to the Manager Dashboard, which shows your store's key metrics at a glance.

### 2.3 Web Admin Portal

You also have access to the web portal at **admin.luckystop.cliffindus.com** for more detailed reporting and management. Log in using your phone number + OTP + PIN.

### 2.4 Security

- Never share your PIN with employees or customers.
- Log out of the web portal when not in use.
- Enable biometric login on your phone for convenience.
- Immediately contact HQ if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 4. Order Lists — Procurement Management

The Order List is your digital procurement tool. Instead of paper lists, you maintain a live, shareable order list that tracks what items your store needs, what has been ordered from suppliers, and what has arrived.

### 4.1 Understanding the Order List

Each store has one **active order list** at a time. When you close a list, it moves to history and you can open a new one. A list contains individual **items**, each with:
- Item name.
- Quantity needed.
- Category (e.g., Frozen Foods, Beverages).
- Priority (Urgent, Normal, Low).
- Notes.
- Status (Pending → Ordered → Received).

### 4.2 Opening a New Order List

If your store has no active list:
1. Navigate to **Order List** in the bottom navigation.
2. Tap **Open New List**.
3. A new order list is created with an auto-generated name based on the date.

### 4.3 Adding Items to the List

**Manual entry:**
1. On the active order list, tap **+ Add Item**.
2. Type the item name — the app will suggest matching items based on your history.
3. Set:
   - **Quantity:** How much you need (e.g., "2 cases," "6 units").
   - **Category:** Select from the available categories.
   - **Priority:** Urgent (need immediately), Normal, or Low.
   - **Notes:** Any specific notes for this item.
4. Tap **Add Item**.

**From employee requests (see Section 5):** Items accepted from employee item requests are automatically added to the active order list.

### 4.4 Managing Item Statuses

As you work through procurement, update each item's status:

| Status | When to Use |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Item is on the list, not yet ordered |
| **Ordered** | You have placed the order with a supplier |
| **Received** | Item has arrived at the store |
| **Removed** | Item was removed from the list (no longer needed) |

To change status:
1. Tap the item.
2. Select the new status.

### 4.5 Editing an Item

Tap any item to open its detail view. Tap **Edit** to modify:
- Name, quantity, category, notes, or priority.
- Tap **Save**.

### 4.6 Removing an Item

Swipe the item left and tap **Remove**, or open the item and tap **Remove Item**.

### 4.7 Reordering Items

Long-press any item to enter drag-reorder mode. Drag items to rearrange them in order of priority.

### 4.8 Printing the Order List

Tap the **Print** button (printer icon) at the top of the active order list to generate a formatted PDF of everything on the list.

The PDF includes:
- Lucky Stop header with store name, list name, and the date printed.
- Items grouped by status: **Urgent**, **Needed**, **Ordered**, **Received** — each with a checkbox column for paper mark-off.
- A barcode under each item that has a matching product in your store's catalog, so you can scan directly off the printout at the supplier.
- A summary count at the top (total items, how many in each status group).

**Two output options appear when you tap Print:**

- **Print Directly** — opens the device's native print dialog (AirPrint on iPhone, system print on Android). Send straight to a connected or wireless printer.
- **Share as PDF** — saves the PDF and opens the share sheet so you can send it via email, WhatsApp, Messages, or save to Files.

> No internet connection is needed to share the PDF once it's generated — useful if you're in a back office with poor signal.

### 4.9 Closing a List

When all items have been ordered and/or received:
1. Tap the **···** menu at the top of the list.
2. Tap **Close List**.
3. Optionally add closing notes.
4. Tap **Close**.

The closed list moves to History, and **a new active list opens automatically** in the same action — your store is never left without an active order list.

**Note:** Closing a list does not delete it — it remains in history for review.

### 4.10 Standing Order Instructions

Each store can keep a short standing note (up to 300 characters) pinned to the top of the order list screen — for anything a supplier or the next shift should always see (e.g., delivery-day reminders, a preferred vendor contact, dock-access instructions). Tap the instructions banner → **Edit** → type your note → **Save**. It persists across closed/reopened lists until you change it.

### 4.11 Viewing List History

Tap **History** (or the clock icon) at the top of the Order List screen to see all previously closed order lists. Each historical list shows:
- List name and dates (opened/closed).
- Total items and their final statuses.
- Closing notes.

### 4.12 Restoring Items from a Closed List

If items from a previous list are still needed:
1. Go to **History** → select the closed list.
2. Select the items you want to restore.
3. Tap **Restore to Active List**.
4. Selected items are added to the current active list.

## 5. Employee Item Requests — Reviewing

Employees can submit requests for items they notice are low in stock or that customers have requested. As manager, you review these requests and decide whether to add them to the active order list.

### 5.1 Seeing Pending Requests

A badge on the **Requests** tab (or your dashboard's Pending Requests count) indicates how many employee item requests are waiting for your review.

### 5.2 Opening the Requests Screen

Navigate to **Requests** → **Stock** tab (if not already on it).

You will see a list of pending requests, each showing:
- Which employee submitted it.
- Date submitted.
- Request type (Low Stock or Customer Request).
- Number of items in the request.
- Any note from the employee.

### 5.3 Reviewing a Request

Tap a request to open it. You will see each individual line item:
- Item name.
- Quantity needed (as specified by the employee).
- Category.
- Notes from the employee.

For each line item, you choose:
- **Accept:** The item will be added to the active order list. If there is no active list, one will need to be opened first.
- **Reject:** The item will not be added. You can optionally select a rejection reason and add a note for the employee:
  - **No Supplier** — We don't have a supplier for this item.
  - **Out of Budget** — Cannot order this currently.
  - **In Stock** — Item is already in stock.
  - **Duplicate** — Already on the order list.
  - **Other** — Provide a custom note.

### 5.4 Submitting Your Review

After setting Accept/Reject for each line:
1. Optionally, select a target **Order List** for the accepted items (defaults to the current active list).
2. Tap **Submit Review**.
3. Accepted items are added to the order list. The employee is notified of your decision.

### 5.5 Viewing Past Requests

Switch to the **History** tab in the Requests screen to see all previously reviewed requests and their outcomes.

### 5.6 Rejection Log

View items that have been rejected (across all employee requests) in the **Rejected Items** log. This helps identify patterns — if employees repeatedly request items that get rejected, discuss the reason with your team.

## 6. Store Alerts — Operations

Store Alerts are maintenance and supply issues employees flag for store-level operational attention (product requests from customers have their own **Products** tab — see Section 13).

### 6.1 Types of Store Alerts

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Low Stock Alert** | "We're out of price stickers," "Running low on cups" |
| **Store Supplies** | "Need new mop heads," "Paper towels" |
| **Work Order** | "Refrigerator 2 is not cooling," "Light fixture out" |

Each alert also has a priority: **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.

### 6.2 Viewing Store Alerts

Navigate to **Requests** → **Alerts** tab. You will see all pending and acknowledged alerts for your store, sorted by priority. If you manage more than one store, a hint indicates when another of your stores also has pending items waiting.

### 6.3 Acknowledging a Request

When you have addressed a request:
1. Tap the request.
2. Review the details (type, priority, submitting employee, notes).
3. Add an **acknowledgment note** (what action you took, e.g., "Ordered replacement parts," "Purchasing next week").
4. Tap **Acknowledge**.

Acknowledged requests remain in history for your records.

### 6.4 Escalating High-Priority Requests

For high-priority requests (e.g., a broken refrigerator), use the Support Ticket system in the Admin Portal to notify Lucky Stop HQ or the Developer if the issue requires platform or infrastructure support.

## 7. Offers — Creating and Managing Promotions

As a Store Manager, you can create offers specific to your store that will appear to customers who shop at your location.

### 7.1 Accessing Offers

Navigate to **Offers** in the bottom navigation. You will see:
- **Active offers:** Currently live promotions at your store (and platform-wide offers from HQ).
- **My Store's Offers:** Offers you have created.
- **History:** Expired offers you can reuse.

### 7.2 Creating a Store Offer

1. Tap **+ New Offer**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title:** Clear, appealing offer name (e.g., "Weekend Grocery Bonus").
   - **Description:** More detail about the offer (optional).
   - **Category:** Apply the offer only to a specific product category, or leave blank for all categories.
   - **Bonus Rate:** The additional cashback percentage (e.g., enter 0.05 for 5% bonus on top of normal rates).
   - **Deal Text:** Short promotional text (max 40 characters, e.g., "Double points this weekend!").
   - **Start Date / End Date:** Set the promotion window.
   - **Offer Image:** (Optional) Add an image for the offer card.
3. Tap **Create Offer**.

Your offer is immediately visible to customers shopping at your store, and a push notification is sent to all customers announcing the new promotion.

### 7.3 Editing an Offer

Tap an active offer → tap **Edit**. You can modify all fields except the store assignment (your offers are always tied to your store only).

### 7.4 Deactivating an Offer

Tap the offer → tap **Deactivate**. The offer is immediately removed from customer view.

### 7.5 Reusing an Expired Offer

Go to **History** → tap an expired offer → tap **Reuse Offer**. Set new dates and modify as needed.

## 9. Transactions — Reviewing Store Activity

### 9.1 Viewing Your Store's Transactions

In the web portal, navigate to **Transactions**. In the mobile app, your home dashboard shows recent transactions.

The Transactions view shows all qualifying purchases at your store with:
- Date and time.
- Customer (name or identifier).
- Employee who processed it.
- Purchase amount.
- Points awarded.
- Status.

### 9.2 Transaction Statuses

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | Processed normally, points credited. |
| **Pending** | Processing in progress (should resolve quickly). |
| **Flagged** | System flagged for review — requires your attention. |
| **Rejected** | You or HQ rejected this transaction. |

### 9.3 Reviewing Flagged Transactions

Flagged transactions appear prominently in the transactions list and may be shown with a warning indicator in the dashboard. To review:

1. Tap the flagged transaction.
2. Review the receipt image uploaded by the employee.
3. Check the purchase amount and details.
4. Choose:
   - **Approve:** Transaction stands, points are confirmed.
   - **Reject:** Transaction is invalid — points are reversed.
5. Tap your decision.

**Common reasons transactions are flagged:**
- Unusually high purchase amount.
- Suspicious transaction velocity.
- Receipt image quality issues.

### 9.4 Rejecting a Transaction

If you determine a transaction is fraudulent or erroneous:
1. Tap the transaction.
2. Tap **Reject Transaction**.
3. The transaction status changes to Rejected and the points are reversed.

## 10. Scheduling — Staff Schedule Management

### 10.1 Understanding the Schedule System

The scheduling feature tracks your store's weekly shift structure. Each employee has a shift template (their recurring schedule), and the system generates a roster based on these templates.

**Shift Types:**
- **Opening:** First shift of the day.
- **Middle:** Mid-day shift.
- **Closing:** Final shift of the day.

**Days of Week:** Monday through Sunday.

### 10.2 Viewing the Store Schedule

Navigate to **Schedule** in the bottom navigation (Manager view). You will see:
- **Today's Roster:** Who is scheduled to work today.
- **Weekly Schedule:** Full week view.
- **Shift Requests:** Time-off and fill-in requests from employees.

### 10.3 Assigning a Shift to an Employee

1. Navigate to **Schedule** → tap **Assign Shift**.
2. Select:
   - **Employee:** Choose from your store's staff.
   - **Day of Week:** Which day this shift applies (for the recurring template).
   - **Shift Type:** Opening, Middle, or Closing.
   - **Start Time / End Time:** Shift hours.
3. Tap **Save Shift**.

Shifts are recurring by default (appear on the same day each week). Employees can see their schedule in the mobile app.

### 10.4 Removing a Shift

1. Navigate to **Schedule** → find the shift.
2. Tap **Remove Shift** or swipe to delete.
3. Confirm the removal.

### 10.5 Handling Shift Requests

Employees can submit two types of shift requests:
- **Time Off:** Employee requests to not work on a specific date.
- **Fill In:** Employee is willing to take an additional shift on a specific date.

To review pending requests:
1. Navigate to **Schedule** → **Shift Requests** tab.
2. Tap a request to review details (employee, date, shift, notes).
3. Tap **Approve** or **Deny**.
4. The employee is notified of your decision.

### 10.6 Viewing a Specific Day's Roster

Tap any day in the weekly schedule view to see exactly who is scheduled to work that day, with their shift type and hours.

### 10.7 Available Shifts (Vacancies)

If shifts are unassigned or an employee's time-off is approved, the shift may appear as a vacancy visible to employees who want to pick up extra hours.

## 11. Store Chat

The store team chat is a real-time messaging channel for all staff assigned to your store. Use it for quick team communication: shift updates, stock alerts, daily briefings.

### 11.1 Accessing Chat

Navigate to **Chat** in the bottom navigation.

### 11.2 How Chat Works

- All staff assigned to your store (employees + you) can see and send messages.
- Messages show the sender's name, role, and timestamp.
- There is one chat channel per store — there are no private or direct messages.

### 11.3 Best Practices for Chat

- Use chat for operational updates: "We're out of regular unleaded, directing customers to diesel" or "I need someone to cover 6PM – close tonight."
- Keep messages professional.
- Check chat at the start of each shift for any updates from the previous shift.

## 12. Hot Food Management

If your store has a hot food program, you can manage the available menu items and monitor live orders through the app.

### 12.1 Accessing Hot Food

Tap **Hot Food** in the menu. This section is only visible to Admin-level roles and is only active if your store has hot food enabled.

### 12.2 Hot Food Catalog

The catalog lists all items that can appear on the customer-facing menu. For each item you can:
- **Enable or disable** it at your store — disabled items do not show to customers.
- Set the **estimated preparation time** (shown to customers when ordering).

### 12.3 Live Orders

Open **Hot Food Orders** to see all pending customer orders in real time. Orders display the customer name, item(s), and time placed.

Your employees manage the order states (In Progress → Ready → Complete), but you can monitor and intervene if needed.

### 12.4 Things to Note

- If you run out of an item, disable it in the catalog immediately to prevent new orders.
- Customers pay at the counter when picking up — hot food orders are not prepaid in the app.
- Hot food purchases earn loyalty points on the transaction like any other purchase.

## 18. Disputes — Missing Points Reports

Customers can report missing or incorrect points two ways: a generic "Report Missing Points" form, or — for a specific past purchase — a **Dispute This Transaction** button on that transaction's detail view in their app. Both land in the same queue for your store.

**This is a mobile-only feature** — dispute review is not available in the web admin portal for Store Managers (that's a Super Admin/HQ-level view). In the mobile app, tap **Disputes** in the bottom navigation (badge shows pending count).

### 18.1 Reviewing a Dispute

Filter by status (Pending, Approved, Rejected). If the dispute was filed against a specific transaction, its details and receipt photo are shown inline; otherwise you're working from the customer's written description.

### 18.2 Resolving a Dispute

Tap a dispute → choose:
- **Approve:** enter the points/credit amount to award, add a note, and confirm. Points are credited immediately.
- **Reject:** add a note explaining why, and confirm.

Disputes are also reachable from your notification feed — tapping a dispute push jumps straight to the matching entry.

## 20. Troubleshooting

**Problem: I can't see my order list.**
- Ensure you are logged in to the correct store account.
- If no list exists, tap "Open New List."
- Check your internet connection — the order list requires connectivity.

**Problem: Employee item request won't submit — says no active order list.**
- You must have an active order list before accepting employee requests.
- Open a new list first, then process the employee request.

**Problem: An employee says their request wasn't received.**
- Ask them to check if the request shows "Submitted" status in their app.
- Check your Requests → Employee Requests screen and confirm the request appears.
- If not visible, they may have encountered a connectivity error — ask them to resubmit.

**Problem: A transaction is stuck in "Pending."**
- Pending transactions should resolve within seconds. If stuck, ask the employee to check their internet connection.
- Contact support if the issue persists.

**Problem: I can't approve a shift request.**
- Ensure you have manager access to the store the employee belongs to.
- Contact HQ (Super Admin) if your store assignments need adjustment.

**Problem: My offer isn't showing for customers at my store.**
- Verify the start date has passed and end date has not passed.
- Verify the offer is Active (not deactivated).
- Note: It may take a few minutes for the offer to propagate.

**For unresolved issues:** Contact Lucky Stop HQ or submit a support ticket through the Admin Portal.
