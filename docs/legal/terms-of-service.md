## 5. Loyalty Points Program

### 5.1 Earning Points

Customers earn loyalty points through qualifying purchases made at participating Lucky Stop locations. Points are earned as follows:

**Standard Earning Rate:**
- Points are calculated as a percentage of your qualifying purchase amount.
- The base cashback rate is **5%** of the purchase total, subject to modification based on your tier, active promotional offers, and the product category of your purchase.
- The specific rate applied to any transaction is determined at the time of the transaction and may vary.

**Categories:**
Points may be earned on purchases across the following product categories: Groceries, Frozen Foods, Fresh Foods, GAS, Diesel, Hot Foods, and Other.

**Gas Purchases:**
For gas purchases, your store may apply either a percentage-based bonus rate or a per-gallon bonus in cents (depending on your tier and active promotions). The applicable method is determined by the system at the time of the transaction.

### 5.2 Transaction Processing

To earn points on a purchase:
1. Present your Lucky Stop app QR code to the cashier before or at the time of payment.
2. The cashier will scan your QR code and enter the transaction details.
3. The cashier uploads a receipt photograph as required fraud documentation.
4. Points are credited to your account upon successful transaction processing.

Points cannot be earned retroactively on transactions where your QR code was not presented.

### 5.3 Promotional Bonus Points

During promotional periods, bonus points may be offered for specific categories, purchase amounts, or other qualifying criteria. Promotional offers are subject to separate terms displayed within the App and may be time-limited, store-specific, or subject to other restrictions.

### 5.4 Point Value

Loyalty points (also referred to as "credits") have a redemption value determined by the terms of the active redemption options available in the App at the time of redemption. **Points have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash under any circumstances.**

### 5.5 Points Expiration

Points do not expire so long as your account remains active. An account is considered active if at least one qualifying transaction has occurred within the preceding **24 months**. We reserve the right to introduce point expiration policies in the future, provided we give you at least **90 days' advance notice**.

### 5.6 Non-Qualifying Transactions

The following do not qualify for points earning:
- Purchases made without presenting your QR code.
- Purchases of items not offered at Lucky Stop (i.e., purchases from third-party vendors operating on Lucky Stop premises where integration has not been established).
- Any transaction rejected by a store manager for fraud, policy violation, or error.
- Transactions processed by the employee themselves (self-granting is prohibited).

### 5.7 Disputes

If you believe a transaction has been incorrectly processed or that points have been incorrectly credited or debited, you must notify us within **60 days** of the disputed transaction by contacting support@luckystop.cliffindus.com with your transaction details. We will investigate and, if the dispute is upheld, correct your balance accordingly.

After 60 days, we may be unable to investigate or correct the disputed transaction.

|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | Starting tier | Base rate |
| **Silver** | Program threshold | Elevated rate |

### 6.2 Tier Period

Tier qualification is based on points earned within a defined **tier period** (e.g., a calendar year or rolling 12-month window). At the end of each tier period, your tier progress resets. Your tier at the end of a period carries over to the beginning of the next period as a starting position, but your accumulated period points reset.

### 6.3 Tier Benefits

Each tier may come with specific benefits beyond enhanced cashback rates, which may include:
- Exclusive promotional offers.

### 6.4 Tier Downgrade
