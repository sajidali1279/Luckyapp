## 1. Program Participation

### 1.1 Eligibility

The Lucky Stop Loyalty Program is open to individuals who:
- Are **18 years of age or older**.
- Have downloaded the Lucky Stop App and registered an account.
- Have a valid United States mobile phone number.
- Are not currently suspended or banned from the Program.

### 1.2 Enrollment

Enrollment in the Program is completed by downloading the App, completing registration (phone verification + PIN + name), and confirming the 18+ age requirement. There is no enrollment fee.

### 1.3 One Account Per Member

Each person may hold only one (1) loyalty account. Attempts to create multiple accounts are a violation of the Program Terms and will result in all accounts being suspended and all points being forfeited.

### 1.4 Non-Transferability

Accounts, QR codes, and accumulated points are personal to the registered account holder. They may not be transferred, sold, gifted, or assigned to any other person.

### 1.5 Staff Exclusion

Lucky Stop employees, store managers, and administrators may maintain a loyalty account for personal purchases, but may not process transactions for their own account. Any self-granting of points is a serious violation and will result in immediate termination of employment access and potential legal action.

## 2. Earning Points

### 2.1 How to Earn Points

Points are earned by making qualifying purchases at participating Lucky Stop locations. To earn points on a purchase:

1. **Before or at the time of payment**, open the Lucky Stop App and display your QR code.
2. The Lucky Stop cashier will **scan your QR code** using the App.
3. The cashier will enter the purchase details (amount, category) and **upload a receipt photograph** as fraud documentation.
4. Points are **credited to your account** upon successful processing.

**Points cannot be earned retroactively** on purchases where your QR code was not scanned.

### 2.2 What Counts as a Qualifying Purchase

Qualifying purchases are cash or card purchases made directly at a participating Lucky Stop location, processed by a Lucky Stop employee through the App. The purchase must be for goods sold at the Lucky Stop location.

### 2.3 When Points Post to Your Account

Points post to your account immediately upon the cashier completing the transaction in the App. You will see your updated balance in the App as soon as the transaction is confirmed.

### 2.4 Minimum Purchase Requirements

Lucky Stop may establish minimum purchase amounts below which points are not earned. Any minimum thresholds are displayed in the App.

### 2.5 Excluded Purchases

The following do not qualify for points:
- Purchases made without scanning your QR code.
- Lottery tickets, money orders, or financial instruments (subject to store policy).
- Purchases that do not result in a receipt or cannot be documented.
- Purchases processed in error and subsequently reversed.
- Transactions rejected by a store manager.
- Tobacco or age-restricted products where local law prohibits loyalty incentives.

### 2.6 Self-Scan Receipt Feature

Lucky Stop may introduce QR codes printed on receipts that customers can scan to claim their own points without employee assistance. This feature is not yet available at any location; when it is, the App will show it and self-scan receipt points will be subject to the same terms as standard transactions.

## 3. Cashback Rate Structure

### 3.1 Base Rate

Your cashback rate is the rate for your tier (Section 4) shown in the App, credited as loyalty points to your account. One point is worth $0.01, so $1.00 of cashback is 100 points.

*Example: At a 5% rate, a $40.00 grocery purchase earns 200 points (5% × $40.00 = $2.00 in credits).*

The cashback on any one purchase, from every rate, bonus and promotion together, is never more than **10% of the purchase amount**. A per-gallon gas bonus for higher tiers (Section 5.2) is paid in addition.

### 3.2 Category-Specific Rates

Lucky Stop may apply different cashback rates for specific product categories. Category-specific rates may be **higher or lower** than the base rate, based on Lucky Stop's promotional strategy. The rate applicable to each category is displayed in the App.

**Product Categories:**
- Groceries
- Frozen Foods
- Fresh Foods
- GAS (Regular gasoline)
- Diesel
- Hot Foods
- Other

### 3.3 Gas and Diesel Purchases

For GAS and Diesel purchases, Lucky Stop may offer cashback either as a **percentage of the total purchase** (consistent with other categories) or as a **cents-per-gallon bonus** applied to the number of gallons purchased. The method applied depends on your current tier and any active gas promotions. The App will display the applicable gas earning method.

### 3.4 Tier-Based Rates

Your loyalty tier (see Section 4) determines your personal cashback rate; higher tiers earn more. Tier-based rates apply automatically based on your current tier.

### 3.5 Points Calculation

Points are calculated as:

> **Points Earned = Purchase Amount × Applicable Cashback Rate**

Where cashback rate is expressed as a decimal (e.g., 5% = 0.05). For gas per-gallon bonuses:

> **Gas Bonus Points = Gallons Purchased × Cents Per Gallon Bonus**

Points are rounded to the nearest whole number.

## 4. Tier System

### 4.1 Tier Levels

The Lucky Stop Loyalty Program has five (5) tier levels:

| Tier | Cashback Rate | Points Threshold (Per Period) |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | Displayed in App | Starting tier (all new members) |
| **Silver** | Displayed in App | Displayed in App |
| **Gold** | Displayed in App | Displayed in App |
| **Diamond** | Displayed in App | Displayed in App |
| **Platinum** | Displayed in App | Displayed in App |

*Note: The current rates and thresholds are always displayed within the App, read live from Lucky Stop's settings. Lucky Stop may adjust them with advance notice; a change applies to purchases made after it takes effect.*

### 4.2 Tier Period

Tier progression is tracked within a **Tier Period** of six months: January 1 to June 30 and July 1 to December 31, in Central time. At the start of each new Tier Period:
- Your period points counter resets to zero.
- Your tier steps down **one level** (for example, Gold becomes Silver; Bronze stays Bronze). That is your starting tier for the new period.
- You climb again as you earn points in the new period. The App notifies you if your tier stepped down.

### 4.3 Tier Qualification

Your tier level is determined by the total points earned within the current Tier Period. As you accumulate points toward the next threshold, the App displays your progress to the next tier. Tier upgrades take effect **immediately** when the threshold is crossed.

### 4.4 Tier Downgrade

Your tier never goes down during a Tier Period. It steps down one level at the start of the next Tier Period, as described in Section 4.2, whatever your points were. If you missed several periods, it steps down one level for each.

### 4.5 Tier Display

Your current tier is displayed prominently on your home screen in the App, along with:
- Your current period points.
- Points required to reach the next tier.
- Your all-time lifetime points balance.

## 5. Tier Benefits

### 5.1 Enhanced Cashback

Each tier level provides a higher cashback rate than the tier below it, as specified in Section 4.1.

### 5.2 Gas Bonuses

Higher tier members may receive a per-gallon cents bonus on gas and diesel purchases instead of or in addition to the percentage cashback. The applicable bonus is specified in the App for your current tier.

### 5.3 Tier Benefit Claims

Certain tiers unlock a "Tier Benefit" that can be claimed in-store once per tier period. To claim:
1. Visit a participating Lucky Stop location.
2. Ask the cashier to process your tier benefit claim.
3. The cashier will verify your tier status in the App and confirm the claim.
4. The benefit is marked as claimed and is not available again until the next tier period.

The specific benefit associated with each tier is determined by Lucky Stop and displayed in the App. Tier benefits have no cash value.

### 5.4 Priority Catalog Access

Platinum and Diamond tier members may receive early or priority access to high-value catalog redemption items. Details are displayed in the App.

## 6. Bonus Points and Promotional Rates

### 6.1 Limited-Time Offers

Lucky Stop periodically offers bonus points opportunities, including:
- **Category Bonus:** Enhanced cashback rate on purchases in a specific category for a limited time.
- **Amount Bonus:** Bonus points for purchases above a specified threshold.
- **Tier Bonus:** Additional points for specific tier members.
- **Gas Bonus:** Enhanced per-gallon bonus during promotional periods.

### 6.2 Finding Offers

All active offers are displayed in the Offers section of the App, along with:
- Offer title and description.
- Applicable bonus rate or deal text.
- Offer period (start and end dates).
- Applicability (all stores or specific location).

### 6.3 Offer Terms

Each offer is subject to its own specific terms displayed within the offer details. Offers:
- Apply only during the stated promotional period.
- May apply only to specified product categories or purchase amounts.
- May apply at all Lucky Stop locations or only at specific stores.
- Are not combinable unless explicitly stated.
- Cannot be applied retroactively to prior purchases.
- Are subject to change or cancellation at any time.
- Are limited by the 10% cap in Section 3.1: when a purchase reaches the cap, the promotion's extra cashback is reduced first.

### 6.4 Promotions for Some Members

Some promotions are only for certain members, and the App shows you only the ones you can use:
- **A tier and up:** members at a stated tier or higher.
- **New members:** members whose account or first purchase is recent, as stated in the offer.
- **Win-back:** members who have not made a purchase for a stated number of days.
- **Birthday month:** members who have saved their birthday (month and day) in the App, during that month. Saving your birthday is optional.

A promotion may also have a total budget, after which it stops paying and is removed from the App, and a limit on the extra cashback one member can receive from it in a day.

### 6.5 Deals

The App also shows in-store deals (for example, "2 for $5"). A deal is a shelf price set by the store, not a points bonus, and is honored at the register of the stores that carry it while it lasts.

### 6.6 Challenges

Lucky Stop may run challenges: a goal, such as a number of visits or an amount spent in a category within the challenge period, that pays a stated cashback reward when you reach it. Challenges:
- Count only approved purchases made during the challenge period at the stores it names.
- May be for some members only, in the same way as Section 6.4.
- Pay their reward once, unless the challenge says it can be earned again.
- Show your progress in the App. The reward is credited automatically when the purchase that completes the goal is approved. If that purchase is later found to be invalid, Lucky Stop may void the reward.

### 6.7 Refer a Friend

You can invite friends with your personal invite code, found under **Invite friends** in the App.
- A friend who is new to Lucky Stop enters your code when creating their account, or in Invite friends within 7 days of joining and before their first purchase. Each account can use one code, and you cannot use your own.
- When your friend makes a purchase of at least the minimum shown in the App (currently **$10**) within the time shown in the App (currently **30 days**) of giving your code, and that purchase is approved, **you and your friend each receive the bonus shown in the App** (currently **$5** in credits each).
- You can be paid for up to the number of friends a month shown in the App (currently **10**). Your friend still receives their bonus after you reach the limit.
- Bonuses are credits with no cash value, appear in each person's history, and count toward tier progress like other cashback.
- Invites must be genuine: creating accounts for yourself, using someone else's phone number, or any other way of earning a bonus without a real new customer is prohibited (Section 13). Lucky Stop may refuse or reverse such bonuses and restrict the accounts involved.
- To show the referral to both people, the App shows the other person's first name and last initial (for example, "Maria G.").
- Lucky Stop may change the amounts and rules or end the program at any time; the App always shows the current ones, and bonuses already paid are not affected.

## 8. Redeeming Points - In-Store Credits

### 8.1 How In-Store Credit Redemption Works

You may redeem accumulated points for in-store credit against a qualifying purchase at any participating Lucky Stop location:

1. Inform the cashier that you wish to redeem points.
2. The cashier will scan your QR code.
3. You confirm the redemption amount.

### 8.2 Redemption Rules

- You may only redeem points equal to or less than your available balance.
- Redemptions cannot exceed the purchase total.
- Partial redemptions are permitted (redeem some points and pay the remainder).
- Redemptions are processed in whole-point amounts.
- Redemptions cannot be reversed once confirmed by the cashier.

### 8.3 Minimum Redemption

Lucky Stop may establish a minimum redemption amount. If applicable, the minimum threshold is displayed in the App.

### 8.4 Redemption Value

The redemption value of points is displayed in the App. Points have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for cash. The dollar-equivalent value of points is subject to change with advance notice.

## 9. Redeeming Points - Redemption Catalog

### 9.1 The Catalog

The Lucky Stop App maintains a Redemption Catalog of specific reward items available for redemption with loyalty points. Catalog items may include:
- Specific Lucky Stop store products.
- Lucky Stop-branded merchandise.
- Other rewards as determined by Lucky Stop.

Each catalog item displays:
- Item name, description, and relevant details.
- Points cost.
- Availability.

Where a reward is a product sold in the store, its points cost may follow the product's shelf price (100 points for each $1.00, rounded up to the next 25 points), so it changes when the price changes. A store may also offer its own rewards at that store only.

### 9.2 How to Redeem a Catalog Item

1. Browse the Redemption Catalog within the App.
2. Select the desired item and tap **Redeem**.
3. Review and confirm the redemption - points are deducted from your balance immediately.
4. You will receive a **unique redemption code** with an expiration date and time.
5. Visit a participating Lucky Stop location and present the redemption code (displayed in the App) to the cashier.
6. The cashier will confirm the redemption in the App.
7. The item or discount is provided to you.

### 9.3 Redemption Code Expiry

Your redemption code is valid for the period displayed at the time of initiation. **If the code expires before in-store confirmation, the points will be automatically returned to your account.** You may then initiate a new redemption.

### 9.4 Cancellation Before Confirmation

You may cancel a catalog redemption at any time before it is confirmed in-store. To cancel, tap Cancel on the pending redemption in the App. Points are returned to your balance immediately upon cancellation.

### 9.5 Redemption Cannot Be Reversed After Confirmation

Once a cashier has confirmed your catalog redemption in the App, the redemption is final and cannot be reversed.

## 12. Transaction Disputes

### 12.1 Reporting a Dispute

If you believe:
- Points were not credited for a qualifying purchase.
- Points were credited in an incorrect amount.
- Points were incorrectly debited from your account.
- An unauthorized redemption was processed on your account.

Contact us at support@luckystop.cliffindus.com within **60 days** of the disputed transaction with:
- Your account phone number.
- The approximate date and time of the transaction.
- The Lucky Stop location.
- The purchase amount.
- Any receipt documentation you have.

### 12.2 Investigation Process

We will investigate your dispute within **10 business days** of receipt. We may contact the relevant Lucky Stop store manager to review the receipt image and transaction record. If the dispute is upheld, we will credit the correct points to your account.

### 12.3 Time Limit

Disputes not submitted within 60 days of the transaction may not be eligible for investigation or correction.

## 13. Fraud Prevention and Account Integrity

### 13.1 Our Fraud Detection Measures

Lucky Stop uses multiple layers of fraud detection to protect the integrity of the Program:

- **Mandatory receipt images:** Every qualifying transaction requires a receipt photograph uploaded by the processing employee.
- **Receipt hash detection:** Receipt images are cryptographically hashed to detect duplicate receipt submissions.
- **Audit logging:** All significant platform actions are permanently logged, including who processed each transaction, when, and what receipt was uploaded.
- **Role restrictions:** Employees cannot process transactions for their own accounts.
- **Account velocity monitoring:** Unusual transaction patterns trigger review.

### 13.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited and constitute program fraud:

| Prohibited Behavior | Description |
|---|---|
| **Self-granting** | Employees processing transactions for their own account |
| **Phantom transactions** | Processing transactions for non-existent purchases |
| **Receipt forgery** | Altering or fabricating receipt images |
| **Duplicate receipts** | Using the same receipt for multiple transactions |
| **Account sharing** | Using another person's QR code to earn points |
| **Multiple accounts** | Creating more than one account to multiply earnings |
| **Collusion** | Coordinating with an employee for fraudulent points |
| **Points trafficking** | Buying, selling, or trading points or accounts |
| **System exploitation** | Intentionally exploiting bugs or errors for unearned points |

### 13.3 Consequences

Violation of fraud provisions may result in:
- Immediate account suspension pending investigation.
- Permanent termination of account and all associated accounts.
- Forfeiture of entire points balance.
- Reversal of fraudulent transactions.
- Reporting to law enforcement.

## 15. Account Suspension and Points Forfeiture

Your account may be suspended or terminated, and your points balance forfeited, for:
- Violation of any Program Terms.
- Fraudulent activity (as described in Section 13).
- Violation of the Terms of Service.
- Account inactivity (no qualifying transactions for **36 consecutive months**).

### 15.2 Points Upon Termination

Upon account termination (whether by you or Lucky Stop):
- Your accumulated points balance is permanently forfeited.
- No compensation is provided for forfeited points.

### 15.3 Inactivity

If no qualifying purchase is made on your account for **36 consecutive months**, your account will be considered inactive and your points balance will be forfeited with **30 days' advance notice** via push notification and/or email (if provided). You may reactivate your account by making a qualifying purchase within the notice period.
