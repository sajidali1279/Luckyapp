## 3. Information We Collect

We collect several categories of personal information depending on how you interact with the Services.

### 3.1 Account Registration Information

When you create an account, we collect:

| Data Point | Purpose | Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile phone number** | Primary identifier, OTP authentication | Yes |
| **Full name** | Display name, transaction records | Yes (after registration) |
| **4-digit PIN (hashed)** | Account security, transaction authorization | Yes |
| **Recovery email address** | PIN recovery, account security | Optional |
| **Profile photograph** | In-app avatar display | Optional |
| **Date of birth / age confirmation** | Age verification (18+ requirement) | Yes |

### 3.2 Transaction and Loyalty Program Data

When you participate in the Lucky Stop Loyalty Program, we collect:

- **Purchase amount:** The dollar value of your transaction at a Lucky Stop location.
- **Purchase category:** The product category of your purchase (e.g., Groceries, Frozen Foods, Fresh Foods, GAS, Diesel, Hot Foods, Other).
- **Points awarded:** The number of loyalty points credited to your account per transaction.
- **Cashback rate applied:** The applicable cashback percentage at the time of the transaction, including any tier-based or offer-based bonus rates.
- **Transaction timestamp:** Date and time of each transaction.
- **Store identifier:** Which Lucky Stop location the transaction occurred at.
- **Employee identifier:** The Lucky Stop employee who processed your transaction (internal use only, not displayed to customers).
- **Receipt image:** If uploaded by the employee processing your transaction, a photograph of your purchase receipt is stored as fraud prevention documentation.
- **Redemption records:** Records of when and how you redeem loyalty points, including the item redeemed, points spent, and confirming employee.
- **Gas transaction details:** For gas purchases, additional data including gallons purchased, price per gallon, and bonus points calculation.

### 3.3 Device and Technical Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect:

- **Device identifier:** A unique device ID used for push notification delivery.
- **Operating system and version:** iOS or Android version.
- **Push notification token:** A token issued by your device's notification service (Apple Push Notification Service or Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver in-app notifications.
- **App version:** The version of the Lucky Stop app installed on your device.
- **Login timestamps:** Records of when you log in and out of the App.
- **Failed login attempts:** Tracked for security purposes; repeated failures may temporarily lock your account.
- **Biometric authentication preference:** Whether you have enabled Face ID, Touch ID, or device biometric login. We do **not** store your biometric data — this is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

### 3.4 Interaction and Usage Data

- **QR code:** A unique QR code assigned to your account, used by employees to identify your account for transactions.
- **Notification interaction:** Whether you have read or dismissed in-app notifications.
- **Catalog redemption history:** Which rewards you have initiated, pending, or completed redemptions for.
- **Leaderboard position:** Your ranking among loyalty program participants (customers) or among employees at a store, based on transaction volume or ratings.
- **Chat messages:** If you are an employee or manager, messages sent through the in-store staff chat feature.

### 3.5 Information Collected from Staff Users (Employees and Managers)

For Lucky Stop employees, store managers, and administrators, we additionally collect:

- **Store assignment:** Which Lucky Stop location(s) you are assigned to.
- **Role designation:** Your access level within the platform (Employee, Store Manager, Super Admin).
- **Work schedule data:** Shift assignments and schedule requests submitted or managed through the App.
- **Shift request history:** Time-off requests, fill-in requests, and their approval status.
- **Transaction processing records:** Records of customer transactions you processed, including receipt images uploaded.
- **Store request records:** Supply or maintenance requests you have submitted.
- **Employee item requests:** Inventory replenishment requests you have submitted.
- **Performance ratings:** Customer ratings given by customers following transactions you processed (on a 1–5 scale).
- **Order list activity:** Items you added to, marked received, or managed within store procurement order lists.

### 3.6 Business Promotion Information

If you choose to submit a request to advertise your business through the App's Business Promotion feature, we collect:

- Your name and contact phone number as the requesting party.
- Your business name, description, and website (if provided).
- A business image or logo (if provided).

### 3.7 Job Application Information

If you use the App to submit a job application to a Lucky Stop location, we collect:

- Your full name and contact phone number.
- Your email address (optional).
- The position applied for and preferred Lucky Stop location.
- Work availability (full-time/part-time preference and shift preferences).
- Prior experience and personal message (optional).

### 3.8 Information You Do Not Need to Provide

You are not required to provide optional information (recovery email, profile photo, job application details, or business promotion details) to use the core features of the App. However, withholding certain information may limit your ability to use specific features.

### 3.9 Location Information

If you grant permission, we access your device's precise geolocation (via GPS/location services) **only while the App is in the foreground and actively in use** — we do not access or track your location in the background. This is used to:

- Identify the Lucky Stop location nearest you, so the App can show you that store's promotions, banners, and (where offered) hot food ordering.
- Determine whether the nearest store sells age-restricted products, so the App can present the existing age-confirmation step (see Section 12) in the appropriate context.

Your device coordinates are processed **entirely on your device** to calculate distance to Lucky Stop store locations (whose addresses are already public information within the App). We do not transmit your coordinates to, or store them on, our servers. See Section 15 for more detail on how this permission works and how to control it.

## 5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

### 5.1 Providing the Loyalty Program

- Calculating and crediting loyalty points to your account based on qualifying purchases.
- Applying the correct cashback rate based on your tier status, active promotional offers, and purchase category.
- Processing point redemptions for catalog items or in-store credits.
- Maintaining your transaction history for your review and for dispute resolution.
- Displaying your QR code for in-store scanning.

### 5.2 Account Management

- Creating, authenticating, and maintaining your account.
- Verifying your identity when you log in using your phone number and PIN.
- Enabling biometric login convenience features on your device.
- Allowing you to reset your PIN via phone OTP verification.
- Recovering access to your account if you lose access to your phone number (using your recovery email, if provided).

### 5.3 Tier Management

- Tracking your cumulative points to determine your current loyalty tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, or Platinum).
- Applying tier-specific cashback rates and benefits.
- Communicating tier upgrades and benefit availability.
- Resetting tier progress according to the program's tiering period.

### 5.4 Communications and Notifications

- Sending push notifications about new promotions, offers, and Lucky Stop news.
- Notifying you of activity on your account (e.g., points received, redemption confirmation, catalog redemption status).
- Delivering time-sensitive operational notifications (e.g., your pending redemption is about to expire).
- Communicating via in-app notifications for employee and manager operational updates.

### 5.5 Fraud Prevention and Security

- Storing receipt images as documentation for each points-granting transaction.
- Detecting and preventing duplicate receipt submissions.
- Monitoring for suspicious activity patterns that may indicate fraudulent use.
- Locking accounts after repeated failed PIN attempts.
- Maintaining audit logs of all significant actions taken within the platform.

### 5.6 Platform Operations

- Enabling store managers to manage their store's offers, banners, schedules, and inventory order lists.
- Facilitating communication between employees and managers through the in-store chat feature.
- Allowing employees to view their schedules and submit shift requests.
- Providing managers and administrators with analytics and reporting tools.

### 5.7 Business Promotions

- Reviewing your business promotion request and, if approved, displaying your business advertisement to Lucky Stop app users.
- Contacting you regarding your promotion request.

### 5.8 Career Applications

- Routing your job application to the relevant Lucky Stop location manager for review.
- Communicating application status updates.

### 5.9 Leaderboard and Community Features

- Displaying your position on the customer loyalty leaderboard (by tier and points volume).
- Displaying employee performance rankings to store managers.

### 5.10 Legal Compliance

- Maintaining records required by law or regulation.
- Responding to lawful requests from government authorities or law enforcement.
- Enforcing our Terms of Service and Loyalty Program Terms.

### 5.11 Analytics and Improvement

- Understanding aggregate usage patterns to improve App features and performance.
- Monitoring system health and diagnosing technical issues.
- Generating business intelligence reports for store operators regarding transaction volumes, popular categories, and customer engagement.

### 5.12 Location-Based Features

- Determining which Lucky Stop location is nearest you, to tailor the promotions, banners, and (where available) hot food ordering shown on your home screen.
- Determining whether an age-confirmation step should be presented because the nearest store sells age-restricted products.

We do not use your location for advertising targeting outside the App, and we do not sell or share your location data with third parties.

## 15. Location Information

The App may request access to your device's precise location (GPS/location services), governed by the iOS and Android system permission prompts.

### What We Access

- **Precise device coordinates**, requested via your device's location services, only while the App is open and in the foreground (`expo-location`'s "when in use" permission). We do not request or use "always allow" background location access.

### Why We Request It

- To identify the Lucky Stop location nearest you and tailor the home screen's promotions, banners, and hot food ordering availability to that store.
- To determine whether the nearest store carries age-restricted products, so the App can show the existing age-confirmation step in the right context.

### How It's Processed

Your coordinates are used **entirely on your device** to calculate distance to Lucky Stop store locations (whose addresses are already public within the App). They are never transmitted to or stored on our servers, and are not retained after the calculation completes — see Section 9 for our data retention practices generally.

### Your Control

- The first time a location-based feature is used, your device will prompt you to allow or deny location access. You may decline.
- You can grant, deny, or revoke location permission at any time through your device's settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Lucky Stop; Android: Settings → Apps → Lucky Stop → Permissions → Location).
- Declining or revoking location permission will not affect your ability to use core app features. Without it, the App defaults to showing all Lucky Stop locations rather than automatically detecting the nearest one, and you will not see the automatic age-confirmation step tied to a detected nearby store (age verification collected at registration, per Section 3.1, still applies).

## 16. Security Measures

We take data security seriously and implement the following measures to protect your personal information:

### Technical Safeguards

- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security) 1.2 or higher.
- **Encryption at rest:** Sensitive data, including PIN hashes and stored credentials, is encrypted at rest in our database.
- **PIN hashing:** Your 4-digit PIN is never stored in plain text. It is hashed using bcrypt with appropriate salt rounds before storage.
- **JWT authentication:** API access is controlled using short-lived JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored in your device's secure storage (SecureStore).
- **Biometric storage:** Biometric authentication data is processed entirely by your device's operating system. We store only a flag indicating whether you have enabled biometric login.
- **Role-based access control (RBAC):** All API endpoints enforce role-based access controls. Employees cannot access manager data; managers cannot access other stores' data; and so on.
- **Receipt image hashing:** Receipt images are hashed to detect and prevent duplicate receipt submissions.

### Organizational Safeguards

- Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.
- All significant actions taken within the platform are recorded in an immutable audit log, including who took the action, when, and what was changed.
- Our backend infrastructure is hosted on Render's SOC 2-certified platform.

### Limitations

No security system is impenetrable. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee that unauthorized parties will never be able to defeat our security measures. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your PIN and for any activity that occurs under your account.

If you suspect your account has been compromised, please change your PIN immediately through the App and contact us at support@luckystop.cliffindus.com.
