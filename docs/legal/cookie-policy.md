## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, remember your preferences, and provide analytics to site operators.

This Cookie Policy applies to the Lucky Stop admin portal only. The Lucky Stop mobile app does not use cookies — it uses secure tokens stored on the device.

---

## What Cookies We Use

### 1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the admin portal to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `auth-token` | Stores your authentication session so you remain logged in | Session (cleared on logout) |
| `csrf-token` | Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |

These cookies do not collect personal information beyond what is necessary to maintain your session.

### 2. Functional Cookies (Local Storage)

The admin portal uses browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to remember your preferences:

| Key | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `auth-store` | Stores your login state and user role so you stay logged in across browser tabs | Until logout or token expiry |
| `preferred-theme` | Remembers your display preferences if applicable | Persistent |

### 3. Analytics Cookies

At this time, the Lucky Stop admin portal does **not** use third-party analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics). Usage data is collected server-side through API request logs and is not shared with advertising networks.

### 4. Third-Party Cookies

The admin portal does not embed third-party advertising, social media widgets, or tracking pixels. No third-party cookies are set.

## How to Manage Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

**Note:** Disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in to the admin portal.

You can also clear all cookies for the admin portal by logging out — this removes the session cookie immediately.
