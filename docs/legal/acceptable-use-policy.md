# Acceptable Use Policy

**Lucky Stop Loyalty Platform**  
**Operated by:** Cliff Industries (sksajidali1279@gmail.com)  
**Effective Date:** May 2026  
**Applies to:** All users of the Lucky Stop platform - store operators, managers, employees, and customers

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines what users of the Lucky Stop Loyalty Platform may and may not do. It applies alongside the Terms of Service (for customers) and the Merchant Agreement (for store operators and their staff).

Violations of this policy may result in immediate account suspension, permanent termination, and where applicable, legal action.

---

## 2. Prohibited Uses - All Users

The following are prohibited for every user regardless of role:

### Fraudulent Activity
- Scanning a loyalty QR code without a corresponding real purchase
- Submitting a receipt photo that does not correspond to the transaction being recorded
- Submitting a modified, duplicate, or fabricated receipt
- Colluding with another user to artificially inflate a points balance
- Creating multiple accounts to claim multiple welcome bonuses or tier benefits

### Account Misuse
- Sharing login credentials with another person
- Logging into another person's account without their explicit permission
- Attempting to access areas of the platform not authorized for your role
- Impersonating another user, employee, or store

### System Abuse
- Attempting to reverse-engineer, decompile, or extract source code from the app or API
- Sending automated requests to the API at volumes that degrade service for other users
- Attempting to exploit security vulnerabilities rather than reporting them responsibly
- Interfering with, disrupting, or attempting to disable the platform's infrastructure

### Harmful Content
- Uploading images that contain illegal content, nudity, hate speech, or content promoting violence
- Using the chat, offer, or banner features to harass, threaten, or discriminate against any person
- Uploading content that infringes on intellectual property rights

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses - Store Operators and Employees

In addition to Section 2, the following are prohibited for store operators, managers, and employees:

- Granting loyalty points to transactions that did not occur at a Lucky Stop location
- Granting points in exchange for anything other than qualifying in-store purchases
- Processing a redemption code for a product not specified in the code
- Using the platform's offer or banner features to advertise products or services not related to the store's legitimate business
- Retaliating against an employee for submitting shift or item requests through the platform
- Creating employee accounts for individuals who are not employed by the store

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses - Customers

In addition to Section 2, the following are prohibited for customers:

- Transferring or selling loyalty credits or redemption codes to another person
- Attempting to use an expired redemption code through manipulation of the device clock or other means
- Submitting a business promotion request that is false, misleading, or promotes an illegal product or service
- Rating an employee based on factors unrelated to the service received (e.g., to manipulate leaderboard rankings maliciously)

---

## 5. Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in the Lucky Stop platform:

- **Do not** exploit it, share it publicly, or use it to access data you are not authorized to view
- **Do** report it to sksajidali1279@gmail.com with a description of the issue and steps to reproduce it
- Cliff Industries will investigate and respond within 5 business days

Responsible disclosure will not result in any penalty or account action.

---

## 6. Enforcement

Cliff Industries reserves the right to:

- **Investigate** any account or activity suspected of violating this policy
- **Suspend** an account immediately if a violation is confirmed or credibly suspected, without prior notice
- **Terminate** an account permanently for serious or repeated violations
- **Recover** points or credits obtained through fraudulent means
- **Report** confirmed fraud or illegal activity to the appropriate authorities

Store operators are responsible for the conduct of their employees on the platform. Repeated employee violations may result in the store's subscription being terminated under the Merchant Agreement.

To report a violation of this policy:  
Email **sksajidali1279@gmail.com** with the subject line "AUP Report" and a description of the incident.

Reports are reviewed confidentially. Cliff Industries does not disclose the identity of individuals who submit good-faith reports.

Cliff Industries may update this policy at any time. Significant changes will be communicated to active subscribers and users via email or in-app notification with at least 14 days' notice.
