## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

**"App"** means the Lucky Stop mobile application available on iOS and Android operating systems, through which customers interact with the Loyalty Program.

**"Admin Portal"** means the web-based administrative dashboard accessible at admin.luckystop.cliffindus.com, through which Store Operators manage their store's loyalty program configuration, staff, offers, and analytics.

**"API"** means the Lucky Stop backend application programming interface accessible at api.luckystop.cliffindus.com.

**"Audit Log"** means the immutable record of all significant actions taken within the Platform by any user, maintained by the Provider for security and compliance purposes.

**"Cashback Rate"** means the percentage of a qualifying purchase amount that is credited to a customer's loyalty points balance.

**"Customer Data"** means personal information of customers who interact with the Platform in connection with the Merchant's store(s), including but not limited to names, phone numbers, transaction history, and points balances.

**"Dev Cut"** means the percentage of each qualifying transaction that is allocated to the Provider as a platform usage fee, as specified in Schedule B.

**"Loyalty Program"** means the points-based customer rewards program operated through the Platform on behalf of the Merchant.

**"Merchant Store"** means each physical Lucky Stop store location registered under this Agreement, as listed in Schedule A.

**"Monthly Subscription Fee"** means the fixed monthly fee payable by the Merchant for each active Merchant Store, as specified in Schedule B.

**"Platform"** means the Lucky Stop Loyalty Platform in its entirety, including the App, Admin Portal, API, and all associated backend infrastructure operated by the Provider.

**"Points" or "Credits"** means the digital loyalty rewards units earned by customers through qualifying purchases and redeemable for catalog items or in-store credits.

**"Qualifying Transaction"** means a purchase made at a Merchant Store by a customer who presents their unique Lucky Stop QR code, resulting in the granting of loyalty points.

**"Receipt Image"** means the photograph of a purchase receipt captured and uploaded by a Merchant employee as mandatory documentation for each Qualifying Transaction.

**"Staff Account"** means a Platform account created for a Merchant's employee, store manager, or super administrator user.

**"Subscription Period"** means each monthly period during which the Merchant is subscribed to the Platform services.

**"Term"** means the duration of this Agreement as specified in Section 18.

## 5. Transaction Fee Structure

### 5.1 How the Dev Cut Works

The Platform's loyalty program is funded by the total cashback cost borne by the Merchant on each Qualifying Transaction. The total cost to the Merchant is the sum of:

- **Customer cashback:** The percentage of the purchase amount credited to the customer's points balance.
- **Dev Cut:** The Provider's platform fee on the same transaction.

The combined rate borne by the Merchant is specified in Schedule B.

### 5.2 Example Calculation

*(Example only — actual rates are specified in Schedule B)*

For a $100 qualifying purchase:
- Customer receives 5% cashback = 500 points ($5.00 value to the customer).
- Provider's Dev Cut = 2% = $2.00.
- Total cost to Merchant = $7.00 on a $100 sale.

The Merchant's obligation is to honor point redemptions by customers when customers present valid redemption requests in-store.

### 5.3 Redemption Costs

When a customer redeems points for in-store credit, the Merchant absorbs the cost of the redemption (i.e., provides the product or discount equivalent to the redeemed points value). Redemption costs are inherent to operating a cashback loyalty program and are borne entirely by the Merchant.

## 9. Service Level Agreement

### 9.1 Uptime Commitment

The Provider commits to maintaining API availability of at least **99.5% uptime** measured on a monthly basis, excluding scheduled maintenance windows and events outside the Provider's reasonable control ("Uptime SLA").

**Uptime Calculation:**
Uptime % = (Total minutes in month - Downtime minutes) / Total minutes in month × 100

Downtime is defined as periods where the Platform API is returning errors to more than 50% of requests for more than 5 consecutive minutes.

### 9.2 Scheduled Maintenance

The Provider will notify the Merchant of scheduled maintenance windows at least **48 hours** in advance via the Admin Portal or email. Scheduled maintenance windows will, where possible, be scheduled during off-peak hours (2:00 AM – 6:00 AM local time).

### 9.3 Incident Response

| Severity | Definition | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Platform completely unavailable | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| **High** | Core features unavailable (transactions, scanning) | 2 hours | 8 hours |
| **Medium** | Non-critical feature impaired | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| **Low** | Minor issues, cosmetic bugs | 1 business day | 5 business days |

### 9.4 SLA Credits

If the Provider fails to meet the Uptime SLA in any calendar month, the Merchant is eligible for a service credit as follows:

| Monthly Uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.4% | 10% of that month's Subscription Fee |
| 98.0% – 98.9% | 25% of that month's Subscription Fee |
| Below 98.0% | 50% of that month's Subscription Fee |

Credits are the Merchant's sole remedy for SLA failures and will be applied to the next month's invoice. Credits do not accrue interest and have no cash value.

### 9.5 Exclusions

The SLA does not apply to downtime resulting from:
- Scheduled maintenance with prior notice.
- Force majeure events.
- The Merchant's misuse of the Platform or actions of Merchant's personnel.
- Third-party service failures beyond the Provider's reasonable control (e.g., Firebase, Cloudinary, Render infrastructure outages).
- Denial-of-service attacks or other security events beyond the Provider's reasonable control.
