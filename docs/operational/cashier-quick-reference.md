# Lucky Stop — Cashier Quick Reference

> **Print this and keep it at the register.** This covers the full scan flow and common issues.

---

## Standard Transaction — 5 Steps

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1** | Ask customer to open their Lucky Stop app and show their QR code |
| **2** | In your app, tap **Scan Customer QR** and point your camera at their QR |
| **3** | Enter the **purchase total** in dollars (e.g., 12.50 for $12.50) |
| **4** | Tap **Take Receipt Photo** — photograph the receipt clearly |
| **5** | Tap **Grant Points** — done. Customer sees credits instantly |

> **Receipt photo is mandatory. You cannot skip it. The system blocks submission without it.**

---

## Redeeming a Reward

When a customer wants to use their credits for a free product:

1. Customer shows you a **redemption code** on their app screen
2. In your app, tap **Redeem Code** and enter the code shown
3. Confirm the item matches what the code says
4. Hand over the item — no charge

> Redemption codes **expire in 15 minutes**. If it's expired, the customer needs to generate a new one.

---

## Common Issues

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| QR code won't scan | Ask the customer to increase screen brightness, or tap **Enter Code Manually** and type the 6-digit code shown below their QR |
| App says "Customer not found" | The QR may be outdated — ask the customer to refresh their home screen and show it again |
| Receipt photo rejected | Make sure the full receipt is in frame, well-lit, and not blurry. Retake and resubmit |
| "Invalid redemption code" | Code may be expired (15-min limit). Ask customer to open catalog and generate a new code |
| App is slow or won't load | Check your internet connection. If the issue continues, write down the transaction and notify your manager |

---

## What You Must Never Do

- Do **not** grant points without a receipt photo
- Do **not** share your login with other employees — everyone has their own account
- Do **not** enter an amount different from the actual purchase total
- Do **not** process a loyalty scan on a transaction where no real purchase was made

> These actions are logged and auditable. Violations result in account suspension.
