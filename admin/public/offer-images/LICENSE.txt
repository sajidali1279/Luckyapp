Lucky Stop offer images (made 2026-10-05)

84 images, 12 for each offer category: Groceries, Frozen Foods, Fresh Foods, Gas, Diesel, Hot Foods, Other.
Size 1200 x 600 PNG. The app shows an offer image as a wide slide and as a small square cut from the middle,
so the main picture is always in the centre. There is no text on them: the offer's own title shows next to the image,
in English or Spanish.

Illustrations: Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D, https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
